Questions we get on every call

Before you talk to procurement

What exactly counts as a desk?

One concurrent signed-in dispatcher session. If you run four desks across three shifts, that is four licences, not twelve — the seats hand over with the shift. Supervisors and duty managers get read-only oversight accounts at no charge, and station agents can be given a view-only board link without consuming a desk.

Do we have to replace Sabre or AIMS to use Meridian?

No. Meridian is a decision surface that sits on top of the systems you already operate. We read schedule, crew and maintenance data through supported connectors, and on Network and above we write approved changes back. Most carriers run 30 to 60 days read-only before enabling write-back, so the desk can be compared against the current process without risk.

How long does implementation take?

A Station deployment with a single read connector is typically live in three weeks. Network deployments average six to eight weeks, most of which is spent modelling your contract rules and validating the legality engine against a year of your historical pairings. Control Centre timelines depend on hosting and are scoped before signature.

Does Meridian take operational control?

Never. Meridian proposes, checks and records; a licensed dispatcher commits. Operational control remains with the certificate holder at all times, and every action written back to a host system carries the dispatcher's identity, the timestamp and the reason code for your FAA file.

What happens to our data if we leave?

Your operational history is yours. At any point you can export the full audit record, disruption history and note threads as JSON or CSV through the API or a one-click export. On termination we keep data for 30 days for retrieval, then delete it and provide written confirmation.

Is there a seasonal or peak-day surcharge?

No. Pricing is flat per desk regardless of how many departures you run or how bad the day gets. If you need to add desks for a seasonal peak, they are prorated monthly and can be released at the end of the season.

What are the security and compliance credentials?

SOC 2 Type II, audited annually, report available under NDA. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, hosted in US regions by default with an EU option. We support SAML SSO on Network and above, and single-tenant or on-premises deployment on Control Centre for carriers with hosting restrictions.
