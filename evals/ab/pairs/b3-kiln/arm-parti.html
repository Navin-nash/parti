Why edges run cold

Elements sit in the walls, and the corners of a shelf are further from more of them than the centre is. Most chambers solve this by running hotter overall, which ages elements faster and gives you a wide spread instead of an even one.

The K18 puts elements in the floor and the door as well as the walls, and drives the four zones independently against separate thermocouples. It costs more to build. It is the only reason to switch.
